Additional algorithms to test

Flat, quarter Kelly and robust quarter Kelly are now implemented in the separate bankroll backtest page linked above. Drawdown-constrained and joint portfolio methods remain research candidates. These change allocation, not the predictive model. The present five levels are fixed amounts, not a bankroll-based Kelly portfolio.

  • Fractional and risk-constrained Kelly: compare quarter/half Kelly with a drawdown-constrained allocation. Requires bankroll and total exposure limits.
  • Robust Kelly: optimize against an uncertainty range around probabilities instead of assuming the point estimates are correct.
  • Probability-uncertainty-aware Kelly: account for uncertainty in estimated probabilities when allocating across mutually exclusive outcomes, such as home/draw/away.
  • Portfolio allocation across matches: optimize simultaneous exposure jointly; do not treat every match as having the entire bankroll available.

Completed experiment: flat stakes versus quarter Kelly versus robust quarter Kelly with prespecified parameters. The new page reports ROI, maximum drawdown, turnover and train/test profit by league.
