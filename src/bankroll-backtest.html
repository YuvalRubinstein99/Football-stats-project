How to read these curves

Train curves use in-sample predictions from models fitted on those matches. They can substantially overstate performance. Test covers 2022–23; train contains 2008–22. Raw/calibrated probabilities and equal/recency weights are separate configurations. No OOB curves are presented here.

Robust Kelly uses a fixed probability uncertainty interval, not a validated confidence bound. Fractional stakes are allowed; no bookmaker minimums, taxes, fees or price slippage are modeled. The original repeated test fixture is retained for comparability. Drawdown is measured at daily settlement. Historical actual lineups differ from expected live lineups. There are no historical Winner results.
