Method

OLS predicts both goal targets. Poisson uses two log-link regressions with fixed alpha=0.01, unchanged from the prior baseline. Both use independent-Poisson conversion to H/D/A probabilities. Ordered logit/probit fit away win < draw < home win with two cutpoints and shared slopes. Scaling, team encoding and removal of non-identifiable directions are fitted separately inside each training fold. The full-data ordered models retain all predictors except exact collinearity. Final feature choices are refit on all 19,263 training rows. No OOB claim for regressions; in-sample training profit is omitted. Betting rules and stake thresholds are unchanged and were not optimized here. Test results are exploratory because this season has been examined previously. Actual historical lineups and recorded odds; no fees, bankroll limits or compounding.

References: Training-only preprocessing and validation; ordered logit/probit parameterization.
