Method and limitations

Unpenalized maximum likelihood, trained on away win < draw < home win. Logistic or normal cumulative link with two ordered cutpoints and shared coefficients across cutpoints. Probabilities are direct; no Poisson goal conversion. These models do not predict goal counts. Training-only scaling and rank reduction remove constants and exact collinearity; no feature selection or tuning on test results. Unknown teams have zero dummy features. Train in-sample results are not OOB and are omitted. No strategy optimization; the test season has already been inspected, so these are exploratory comparisons. Historical odds and actual lineups, no fees, compounding or bankroll constraints.

Ordered model reference and parameterization. The local implementation uses an analytic likelihood gradient, finite-difference checks, two starts, convergence checks and prediction/export reconciliation.
