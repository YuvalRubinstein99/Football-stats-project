Home wins versus draw or away wins

Synthetic double chance: odds=1/(1/draw_odds+1/away_odds); split the one-unit stake as effective_odds/draw_odds and effective_odds/away_odds. Either draw or away wins the covered outcome. These are not quoted double-chance prices. Assumes simultaneous recorded odds and fractional stakes, with no fees or rounding.

Use the same binary selection but place a straight away-win bet on not-home picks. Draws lose. P(away)+P(draw) is not the probability that this straight bet wins.
