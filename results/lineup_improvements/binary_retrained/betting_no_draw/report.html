Binary models: weighting and skipping without draw bets

Only straight home/away bets; draws remain in every evaluated sample and lose either bet. No outcome-based exclusions. One bet at most per fixture; ties choose home.

The models predict P(home win) and P(draw or away win). H/A weighting assigns the latter to the away selection as a heuristic score. It is NOT a calibrated away-win probability. Away expected-return filters and stake scores are therefore proxies. Home-only expected-return rules use the genuine model probability of a paying outcome.

Synthetic double-chance rules omitted. Draw-to-home rules duplicate probability and return-score rules when no draw selection exists. Home-only sqrt rule compares against straight-away proxy odds, not the old synthetic draw/away hedge. Legacy market power uses normalized three-way odds but only home/away can be selected. Fixed legacy odds>=4 and score-edge>2% filter retained as an exploratory heuristic, not claimed validated for binary models.

Flat: one unit per strategy selection. Tiers: existing positive-score gate, score=(probability_score*odds-1)/(odds-1); each 1 percentage point advances one tier, capped at five, stakes 1..5 units. Non-positive scores skip. Same-bets flat profit also shown.
