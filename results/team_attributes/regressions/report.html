Predeclared reduced feature sets; smallest encoded dimension within one standard error of minimum mean seasonal log loss, then raw column count. The two-fold standard error is a simplicity heuristic, not a confidence interval.

Raw feature counts include team names as individual columns. Encoded counts include team dummies and missing-value indicators. Ordered models additionally remove exact collinearity.
