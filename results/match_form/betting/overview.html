Historical betting rerun — 2022–23

1,445 fixtures across England, Spain, Germany and Italy. Seven model families, 15 unchanged selection rules and three fixed staking modes. Feature sets were chosen using earlier chronological cross-validation; the main tables use those frozen minimum-CV choices with form allowed.

These are hypothetical historical returns, not money earned. Each row/model is a separate alternative. Do not add their profits together. All amounts below are dollars; ROI is net profit divided by total stakes. Bet365 recorded odds, without fees, taxes, stake rounding or execution costs. The audit/test seasons were previously inspected; rankings are exploratory, not evidence of future profit.
